About HM Foundation

A more compassionate and inclusive Sierra Leone

HM Foundation is a humanitarian initiative committed to supporting vulnerable people, promoting mental wellbeing and creating pathways to education, skills and opportunity.

Our approach goes beyond short-term assistance. We aim to help people regain stability, strengthen resilience and build the capacity to shape their own future.

01

Mission

To uplift vulnerable and underserved individuals through mental health advocacy, humanitarian support and sustainable empowerment initiatives.

02

Vision

A society where every individual has access to mental wellness, humanitarian support and the opportunity to live a dignified and meaningful life.

03

Principle

We serve without discrimination based on religion, ethnicity, gender, disability, political affiliation or social background.
