OUR MISSION

Support that meets people where they are

To uplift vulnerable and underserved individuals through mental health advocacy, humanitarian support, and sustainable empowerment initiatives.

We serve with dignity, compassion and fairness—without discrimination based on religion, ethnicity, gender, disability, political affiliation, or social background.

About HM Foundation
Our vision

A dignified and meaningful life for every person

A society where every individual has access to mental wellness, humanitarian support, and the opportunity to live a dignified and meaningful life.
