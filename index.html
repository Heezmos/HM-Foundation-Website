Who We Are

Humanity first. Dignity always.

HM Foundation is a humanitarian, non-profit initiative founded in Sierra Leone to uplift the less privileged, advocate for mental wellness, and create pathways to education, skills, resilience, and opportunity.

We believe meaningful impact goes beyond temporary charity. Our work is designed to restore hope, strengthen communities, and help people build sustainable, dignified lives.

01

Our Mission

To uplift vulnerable and underserved individuals through mental health advocacy, humanitarian support, and sustainable empowerment initiatives.

02

Our Vision

A society where every individual has access to mental wellness, humanitarian support, and the opportunity to live a dignified and meaningful life.

03

Our Promise

We serve without discrimination based on religion, ethnicity, gender, disability, political affiliation, or social background.

Our Programs

Three ways we turn compassion into action.

Our programs connect immediate humanitarian support with long-term wellbeing and empowerment.

🧠

Mental Health Advocacy & Support

We promote awareness, reduce stigma, encourage early support, and create safer conversations around mental wellbeing.

  • Community awareness campaigns
  • School and youth workshops
  • Psychosocial support referrals
  • Safe-space and resilience initiatives
🤲

Humanitarian Support

We respond to practical needs while protecting dignity and helping vulnerable people regain stability.

  • Food and clothing assistance
  • Education and school materials
  • Health support referrals
  • Support for vulnerable children and families
🌱

Youth Empowerment & Skills

We help young people access practical knowledge, mentorship, confidence, and pathways toward self-reliance.

  • Digital and vocational skills
  • Mentorship and career guidance
  • Leadership development
  • Community service opportunities
